01 / CARTRIDGE

One cartridge, three ways to play

VECPAC A.E. is a Vectrex homebrew cartridge written in Motorola 6809 assembly. The complete image is only 9,255 bytes and combines a 30-level campaign with two standalone arcade modes.

02 / MODES

Choose your mission

1

GAME

The complete 30-level campaign: three cycles of eight Jetpac stages followed by two Killer Queen stages. Score and reserve lives carry across both games, while the difficulty rises from cycle to cycle.

2

MINI GAME JETPAC

A standalone 24-level Vectrex adaptation with four ship designs, precise thrust control, lasers, collectibles, enemies and a dedicated victory screen.

3

MINI GAME KILLER QUEEN

A fixed-screen vector shooter with swaying formations, diving attackers, shields and the Killer Queen encounter, with tighter attack patterns as the run progresses.
